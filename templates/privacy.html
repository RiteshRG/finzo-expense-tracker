Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

Finzo is designed to help you track personal spending and manage your financial habits with confidence. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect the information you share with us while using the Service.

1. Data We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly when creating an account or using the Service, such as your name, email address, password details, transaction amounts, dates, categories, descriptions, and account preferences. We may also collect usage information such as session activity, device details, and general app interaction data needed to improve the experience and maintain security.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide core features such as expense tracking, category summaries, monthly insights, and account access. Your information helps us personalize your experience, maintain the security of your account, respond to support requests, and improve the reliability and usability of the application.

We do not sell personal data to third parties. Any processing is limited to the operation, security, and improvement of the Service.

3. Data Storage

Your financial information is stored in a secure environment using industry-standard protections and access controls. We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the Service, maintain account records, meet legal obligations, or support internal operational needs.

You are responsible for keeping your login information secure and for ensuring that the content you enter into Finzo is accurate and appropriate for your use of the Service.

4. Third Party Services

Finzo may use trusted third-party services to support hosting, authentication, analytics, or communication features. These providers may process limited information on our behalf in accordance with their own privacy practices and applicable security standards.

When using third-party integrations, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place and that the information shared is necessary for the service to function as intended.

5. Your Choices

You may update your account information, manage your preferences, or contact us to request information about your personal data. If you delete your account or stop using the Service, some information may remain in backups, logs, or records required for operational or legal reasons.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your data, or how Finzo handles personal information, please contact the support team through the contact details available within the application or on the project’s official communication channels.
